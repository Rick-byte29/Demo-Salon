Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply within the hour during opening times.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any Book button to message us on WhatsApp, or use the form below. Tell us the service and your preferred day and we will confirm a slot.

Do I need a consultation before colour?

Yes, and it is complimentary. For major colour changes we recommend a short consultation a few days before, so we can plan the right number of sessions.

Will lightening damage my hair?

Every lightening service includes bond protection, and your artist will tell you honestly if your hair needs a rest first.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please let us know at least 24 hours ahead so someone else can take your slot. Bridal bookings carry a small advance that is adjusted in the final bill.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. The Bridal Morning includes a trial session so you can try looks with your artist weeks before the wedding.

Which products do you use?

We work with professional colour and care lines that are ammonia-light and formulated for long-term hair health. Ask and we will show you what is used on your hair.
